# FR-001-010 BeneficiaryDesignations: Beneficiary Designation Gaps and Nuances

## Description
The system shall track beneficiary designations across registered assets and surface gaps and
important nuances (designations that bypass probate, creditor protection).

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Advisor can record beneficiary designations per registered asset (RRSP/RRIF/TFSA/insurance)
   and flag missing/needed ones (per UC-001-002).
2. The system surfaces beneficiary designation gaps in the summary (FR-001-004).
3. The system notes that valid beneficiary designations (and jointly-held assets) bypass probate
   and reduce estate administration fees (FR-001-001), and that certain vehicles (e.g., seg
   funds, insurance) can offer creditor protection.
4. The system records where the designation forms are kept (institution + location only).
