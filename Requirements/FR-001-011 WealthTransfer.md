# FR-001-011 WealthTransfer: Wealth Transfer and Estate Freeze

## Description
The system shall support wealth-transfer and estate-freeze planning: corporate estate freezes
(HoldCo/OpCo), the Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE), the role of life insurance
(Buy-Sell, Capital Dividend Account, estate equalization), and trusts (Alter Ego / Joint
Partner), and flag issues for blended families and intestacy.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. The plan can record corporate structures (HoldCo/OpCo) and a freeze, and note the LCGE
   (approx. $1.25M for 2026; jurisdiction-sensitive, see FR-001-005) available to shelter
   capital gains.
2. The plan can record life insurance roles: funding a Buy-Sell, building a Capital Dividend
   Account, or estate equalization among heirs.
3. The plan can record Alter Ego / Joint Partner trusts for older clients.
4. The system flags blended-family and intestacy risks (no will, or a will that does not reflect
   current family structure) for advisor review.
5. These are planning aids/flags, not legal advice; output is labelled accordingly.
